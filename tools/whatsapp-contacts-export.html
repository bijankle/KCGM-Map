WhatsApp Contacts Export

Pull names and phone numbers out of a WhatsApp group and save them to Excel. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Option 1. Load an exported chat

In WhatsApp open the group, tap the group name, then Export chat, Without media. Drop the .txt or .zip file here. This captures everyone who has posted, joined, been added or left in the chat history.

Drop the chat .txt or .zip here, or click to choose

Option 2. Paste the member list

On WhatsApp Web or Desktop, the line under the group name at the top of the chat lists every member separated by commas. Hover it, select the text, copy, and paste it here. This gets the full current membership, including people who never posted.

Why do some rows have a name but no number?
  • WhatsApp shows your saved contact name instead of the number for people in your phone book, and the export does the same. For those, the number is not in the file.
  • People not in your phone book appear as a number, sometimes with a "~ Name" profile name. Those come through with both.
  • To get numbers for saved contacts, export your phone contacts (Google Contacts or iCloud as CSV) and match on name in Excel with XLOOKUP.
